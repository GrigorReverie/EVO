    How items reach Problem2Action

    1. Add an SOP, email template or ticket template, or import a Rovo document (.md or .docx). Imports start as In review.
    2. Link and tag. Link each template to its SOP, and give SOPs and templates shared tags so they relate to each other and P2A picks the right reply and ticket format.
    3. Optimize with AI: it checks clarity, missing steps and whether templates match their SOP.
    4. Approve. Approved items reach Problem2Action straight away, for everyone using this app. Retired items stay here for reference.

    Import

    The SOP template or the document Rovo gives you. The SOP, its replies and its ticket formats come in together, linked to each other.
